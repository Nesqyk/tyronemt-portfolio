# Panoptes

Reliable webhook routing for Cardano, translating on-chain events into actionable backend triggers.

## Overview

Webhook infrastructure for teams building on the Cardano ecosystem.

## Problem

Custom polling stacks make on-chain automation fragile and operationally expensive.

## Approach

Subscription management, payload validation, and event visibility turn transactions into reliable webhook events.

## Role

Systems design, product direction, and full-stack development.
