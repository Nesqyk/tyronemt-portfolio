# BrokerBuddy

Centralized property management system enabling real estate brokers to track listings, media, and client inquiries.

## Overview

A property operations system for real estate brokers in the Philippines.

## Problem

Property details, media, and buyer inquiries are often fragmented across tools.

## Approach

A single workspace consolidates listing information and client-facing follow-up.

## Role

Product building and full-stack development.
