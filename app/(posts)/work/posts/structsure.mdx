# StructSure

Structural inspection platform combining guided field capture with AI-powered damage tracking.

## Overview

A structural inspection workflow for businesses, infrastructure operators, and licensed engineers.

## Problem

Building condition reports after extreme weather or during routine audits are slow and difficult to verify.

## Approach

Guided field capture, AI crack segmentation, change detection, and an engineer-reviewed dashboard produce compliance-ready reports.

## Role

Founder, product builder, and full-stack developer.
