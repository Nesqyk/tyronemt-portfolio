# Perch

Zero-install campus utility for finding and sharing open study spots in real time.

## Overview

A lightweight utility for students and campus communities.

## Problem

Peak-hour campus congestion makes it hard to find available study locations.

## Approach

Shared links and live availability states provide immediate location context without an app-store download.

## Role

Product direction, interaction design, and full-stack development.
