# WattKoop

AI-assisted energy resilience platform translating grid-risk signals into coordinated community action.

## Overview

A civic-tech coordination layer for Cebu and Visayas communities.

## Problem

Grid stress and brownouts make it difficult for communities and critical facilities to coordinate preparation.

## Approach

AI Risk Heatmaps route localized signals into preparation missions and post-event impact reports.

## Role

Product direction, systems design, and full-stack development.
