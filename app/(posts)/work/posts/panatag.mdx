# Panatag

AI-powered AR memory-listening game that turns heritage places into playable cultural memory encounters.

## Overview

A mobile AR narrative game for Filipino heritage communities.

## Problem

Heritage places and cultural memories are difficult to experience through conventional digital interfaces.

## Approach

Players listen to places, awaken memory fragments, speak with AI Echoes, and restore heritage artifacts.

## Role

Product direction, product building, and full-stack development.
