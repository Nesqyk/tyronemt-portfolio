# PigeonVault

Comprehensive loft management dashboard streamlining pedigree tracking, race results, and OCR-powered workflows.

## Overview

A loft management environment for PigeonVault operators.

## Problem

Pedigree, transfer, billing, and race-result records are complex to maintain manually.

## Approach

OCR document processing and guided forms centralize records and reduce repetitive data entry.

## Role

Product building and full-stack development.
